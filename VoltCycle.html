Designing a Battery Recycling Operations Platform for Safe End-of-Life Processing

Designed a battery recycling operations platform that helps recyclers inspect incoming batteries, manage safety risks, and track dismantling workflows through a streamlined compliance-focused interface.

  • Role UI/UX Designer
  • Timeline 7 Days
  • Tools Figma . Design Systems

Why this project?

This project explored how UX can improve safety-critical industrial workflows where operational mistakes can create real-world risks.

The Problem

Battery recyclers operate in high-risk environments with complex compliance demands, fragmented tools, and life-threatening hazards. There was no unified platform to manage this safely.

How might we help?

“How might we help recyclers process dangerous batteries quickly, safely, and efficiently while ensuring traceability and compliance?”

Goals & Needs

  • Fast Processing -Complete battery assessment in minimal time
  • Safety Guidance -Clear alerts for thermal and chemical risks
  • Step Clarity -Guided dismantling workflows with no ambiguity

Key Challenges & Solutions

01

Large amount of technical information to present clearly without overwhelming operators.

Solution

Used card-based hierarchy to prioritize critical data - status badges, compliance flags, and routing decisions are surfaced first.

02

Safety-critical workflows require instant recognition of hazard levels.

Solution

Implemented warning alert systems with color-coded severity, icons, and mandatory acknowledgment flows.

03

Long dismantling workflows risk operator confusion and missed steps.

Solution

Designed guided step-by-step workflows with progress tracking, checkpoints, and contextual part logging.

Accessibility Considerations

Color Contrast

WCAG AA contrast ratios on all text and UI elements for readability.

Visual Hierarchy

Clear typographic scale – operators can scan information at a glance.

Large Touch CTAs

Minimum 44×44px clickable targets on all action buttons.

Status Beyond Color

Warnings use icons + labels – not color alone – for accessibility.

Consistent Typography

Montserrat across all elements with defined size scale and spacing.

Readable Spacing

Generous line height and padding for dense information layouts.

Final Solution

Workflow Architecture

A horizontal workflow was introduced to visually represent the battery recycling journey from Incoming Battery → Safety & Handling → Dismantling → Reporting.

This helped operators quickly understand their current stage, upcoming actions, and overall process flow at a glance. By simplifying complex operational sequences into a guided linear experience, the interface improved navigation clarity, reduced cognitive load, and supported faster decision-making in safety-critical environments.

Learnings & Reflection

“This project helped me understand how UX design can simplify highly technical workflows where usability directly impacts safety and efficiency. Every design decision — from card hierarchy to alert severity — had real operational consequences.”
01

Safety-first design requires constant trade-offs between information density and clarity.

02

Guided workflows reduce cognitive load more effectively than data-dense dashboards.

03

Accessibility principles directly improve usability in high-stakes environments.
